CAPE Information Technology · Unit 2 · Module 1

Information Management

The thirteen specific objectives build one skill: turning an organisation's scattered records into a well-designed working database. The map follows the order the objectives are taught: six topics, from the language of data to a finished database.

Point at or tab to any card to see its links to objectives in other topics and the reason for each. Select a card to open its notes, key definitions, a worked example, the past paper questions that tested it, and a practice question.

Course learning outcomes served by this module

LO1Organise and manage data, and design and construct a relational database using normalisation, entity-relationship diagrams and SQL.
LO2Document the flow of information within an organisation using data flow diagrams.
LO3Evaluate the impact of data quality and of the database approach on organisational performance.
LO9Research, prepare for and complete Paper 032, applying information-management skills to a given scenario under examination conditions. Module 1 Paper 032 tasks: P032-1 to P032-7. Each topic below names its task.
